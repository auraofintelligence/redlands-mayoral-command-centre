# Research references for Luke's election explorer

Research date: 8 November 2026  
Owner: Luke Nathan Hayes  
Operating model: Luke plus AI companions  
Time zone: Australia/Brisbane

Luke's current direction is a relaxed, serendipitous exploration of a possible mayoral campaign. The primary interface is a browsable collection of media and commentary options, with space to save possibilities. Luke chooses the direction, pace and any dates. The public campaign preview is secondary.

The references below offer background for capabilities Luke may choose to use. They do not create a work programme, review cycle, priority count, publishing cadence or outreach sequence. External election dates and rules are reference information, separate from Luke's personal choices. New personal records begin empty.

## Media discovery and source context

The current directory offers routes into radio, traditional media, new media, public commentators and relevant organisations. An outlet can be a useful discovery route without confirmed coverage of this election. Each entry's public source, coverage evidence and checked date make that distinction visible. Saving it records interest, not consent or a decision to contact it.

Official WA guidance recommends familiarity with council strategies, policies, budgets and meeting records before responding to questions. NDI's campaign-skills resources also distinguish research from plans, timelines, budgets and role descriptions. These are background ideas for source traceability; WA rules and NDI's proposed campaign structures are not imported into this Queensland workspace. [WA campaign preparation](https://www.wa.gov.au/organisation/local-government/election-campaigning), [NDI Campaign Skills 101](https://ndi.org/dcc-campaign101)

The source library preserves a title, public URL, reviewed version or commit, checked date, factual summary and source status. Proposals, prototypes and published work remain distinct. A repository's existence is not evidence of a delivered civic outcome. Luke can follow a source or attach it to a possibility without turning it into a campaign policy.

## Optional dates and personal capacity

NDI recommends a master campaign calendar and a responsible maintainer, with event details, invitation decisions and resource links. That is the source's organisational model, not a schedule assigned to Luke. [NDI Political Campaign Planning Manual, pages 46 to 48](https://www.ndi.org/sites/default/files/Afgh-campaign-planning-manual-ENG.pdf)

WA's candidate-training material asks prospective candidates to consider available time, funds and assistance. Those are optional reflection questions here. WA electoral rules do not govern the Redland election. [WA candidate planning activity](https://www.wa.gov.au/organisation/local-government/activity-3), [WA election campaigning guidance](https://www.wa.gov.au/organisation/local-government/election-campaigning)

A possibility can remain undated. If Luke chooses a date, it records that choice. A sender's proposed meeting time or reply deadline stays part of the request until Luke agrees. An official election reference does not become a personal task merely because it exists. The interface supplies no default daily review, countdown or urgency ranking.

## Optional resources and records

NDI discusses estimating activity costs and comparing expected payments with available resources. That is budgeting background, not a proposed spending allocation. [NDI Political Campaign Planning Manual, pages 52 to 53](https://www.ndi.org/sites/default/files/Afgh-campaign-planning-manual-ENG.pdf)

If Luke chooses to track an expense, proposed, committed and paid amounts describe different facts. Receipts and account information belong in restricted storage. Unknown figures remain unknown, including an unverified statutory cap. A saved row does not file a disclosure or authorise spending. Current Queensland account and disclosure information is available from ECQ. [ECQ campaign bank accounts](https://www.ecq.qld.gov.au/_resource/documents/pdf/election-participants/handbooks,-fact-sheet-and-forms/local/fact-sheets/LG-Fact-sheet-17-Dedicated-campaign-bank-accounts-for-candidates-v.-Mar-26.pdf), [ECQ funding and disclosure overview](https://www.ecq.qld.gov.au/_resource/documents/pdf/election-participants/handbooks,-fact-sheet-and-forms/local/fact-sheets/LG-Fact-sheet-16-Funding-and-disclosure-overview-for-candidates-v.-Mar-26.pdf)

## Drafts and correspondence Luke chooses to develop

The US Election Assistance Commission offers media, events, branding and communications-calendar resources for election administrators. Its organisational examples are background only; it does not prescribe a candidate's communication process here. [EAC Communications 101](https://www.eac.gov/election-officials/communications-101)

A draft can retain its source, rights, open questions and exact version without an assigned publication time. Luke may ask an AI companion to explore, check or revise it. Any external action follows his applicable explicit instruction. A saved draft, wording approval or proposed date is not evidence of a send or post.

OAIC describes political exemptions with particular scopes. That does not establish that every activity of an intending independent candidate is exempt. The workspace keeps actual consent, requests and contact preferences distinct from directory research. [OAIC political parties and elections](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/your-privacy-rights/more-privacy-rights/political-parties-and-elections)

If Luke chooses to preserve an enquiry, record only relevant information and the sender's stated request. An enquiry does not subscribe the person to updates. Public source discovery does not establish permission for automated outreach. No persuasion scores, inferred political beliefs or demographic messaging segments are part of this design.

## Storage choices and their actual state

NDI's security handbook describes restricted sharing, account security and separate backups. Those ideas support an honest description of where records are held. [NDI, Storing Data Securely](https://parties.cyberhandbook.org/topics/storing-data/storing-data-securely)

The public explorer saves chosen records in the current browser and origin. It is not an authenticated private account. The local control-room server and CLI use restricted local files. Google Drive integration source is a separate option; a connector or folder does not prove that an Apps Script portal is deployed. The modes do not automatically synchronise. Report a successful cloud save only after authenticated write and readback.

Sensitive correspondence, contacts, financial data, tokens and private attachments stay out of the public build. Actual connection state, saved records and available exports are useful information. Fabricated activity, projected votes, invented support and progress inferred from generated files are not.

## Official references, separate from personal commitments

ECQ's candidate resources cover nomination, training, finance, advertising and disclosure. Its record-keeping guidance describes records and retention obligations. These are sources to examine when relevant to an action Luke chooses, not a generated personal checklist. [ECQ local-government candidate resources](https://www.ecq.qld.gov.au/election-participants/local-election-participants/candidates), [ECQ record-keeping fact sheet](https://www.ecq.qld.gov.au/_resource/documents/pdf/election-participants/handbooks,-fact-sheet-and-forms/local/fact-sheets/LG-Fact-sheet-08-Record-keeping-requirements.pdf)

ECQ announced the mayoral by-election for 12 December 2026 as a full postal ballot, with the notice and nominations opening on 10 November. Those are external dates. The earlier mention of 11 November is a tentative option only; it is not the campaign's declared launch. Nomination closing time, expenditure cap and postal-return details remain unset until verified from the official notice. [ECQ announcement](https://www.ecq.qld.gov.au/media/2026-media-statements)

Current configuration records no certified nomination. The authoriser name is recorded and the address remains omitted at Luke's instruction. A preparation preview does not establish publication compliance or an obligation to publish. Read current official guidance in the context of any chosen external use. [ECQ authorisation guidance](https://www.ecq.qld.gov.au/_resource/documents/pdf/election-participants/handbooks,-fact-sheet-and-forms/local/fact-sheets/LG-Fact-sheet-03-Authorisation-of-election-material-v.-Mar-26.pdf)

## Limits of the references

The NDI manuals are general and some are older. Their electoral tactics, jurisdictional assumptions and staffing structures are not imported. EAC guidance concerns US election administration. WA guidance supplies background questions, not Queensland law. ECQ and relevant legislation govern Queensland requirements. This research does not claim that Luke has completed those requirements or chosen a campaign schedule.
