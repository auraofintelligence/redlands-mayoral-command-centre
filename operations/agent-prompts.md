# Optional AI exploration prompts

Luke chooses the question, pace and next action. These prompts are available when useful. They do not assign work, establish a schedule, authorise contact or turn an idea into a commitment. Follow Luke's current instructions and existing tool permissions.

## Explore the media landscape

> Help me explore public media and commentary about the Redland mayoral election. Separate confirmed election coverage from general radio, news, community and commentary discovery routes. Give each entry its source link, category, checked date and what the source actually establishes. Do not imply completeness, endorsement or a contact relationship. Show useful filters and options; do not rank people by political susceptibility or prescribe whom I should approach.

## Follow a thread

> Explore this question through the supplied sources. Keep factual claims, opinions, proposals and uncertainty distinct. Show related routes I could choose if interested. Do not assign priorities, due dates or a next action unless I ask for them.

## Capture a possibility

> Help me express this idea clearly and connect it to any relevant public source. Keep dates blank unless I provide one. Label it as a possibility, not an approved action or promise. Do not create recurring work, calendar events or messages from this note.

## Check a source

> Check this statement against its linked evidence. Use primary sources for official election facts. Record the checked date, what the source supports and what remains unresolved. Use Australian English, no em dashes, no minimisation, no aggrandisation and no invented endorsement.

## Explore a draft

> Prepare a factual general draft from the source material I choose. Keep the intended format and open questions visible. Preserve source status, rights and attribution. Do not invent quotes, approved positions, launch dates or commitments. Keep it as a draft unless my instruction explicitly authorises the next action.

## Understand an enquiry

> Read this enquiry that I have chosen to provide. Identify the question, requested format and any date the sender actually supplied. Distinguish their request from my commitment. Suggest factual response options from the supplied evidence without sending anything or subscribing the sender to updates.

## Review what I saved

> Summarise the possibilities I selected, with their source links and any dates I deliberately entered. Do not create an urgency ranking, daily routine or fixed plan. Help me find a thread I might enjoy exploring, and leave the choice with me.

## Inspect a storage choice

> Explain where these records are saved, what access is required and which export or backup options actually work. Keep browser-local, local private and Google storage distinct. Do not describe source code or a folder as a live integration. Do not move, delete or publish existing private records without an instruction that covers that action.
