# Communication options for Luke and AI companions

Luke chooses whether to explore a source, develop a draft, respond to a request or publish something. AI companions can prepare factual material and organise evidence within his current instructions. There is no prescribed outreach sequence, publishing cadence or review queue. The tools in this repository do not send email, post social media or subscribe people.

## Source-backed public information

When preparing public material, use the current configuration and source record: name, office under consideration, nomination status, source links, actual contact availability and authorisation status. Keep external election dates separate from any date Luke deliberately chooses. The earlier mention of 11 November 2026 is a tentative option, not an announced launch.

The current media brief is a preparation document with incomplete authorisation. It does not establish that Luke has decided to publish, that a nomination is certified or that a contact service is connected. Check the actual state before describing any of those things as complete.

## Possible routes

| Route | Something Luke could choose | Context to keep |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Individuals | Read or answer an incoming question | The actual request and any stated contact preference |
| Businesses and community organisations | Explore a public source, ask for factual information or consider an invitation | Source, purpose and any actual response |
| Government and public bodies | Read official material or prepare a procedural question | Authoritative source and any issued reference |
| Traditional media | Follow coverage or consider an interview request | Outlet, journalist, source link and the exact request |
| New media | Explore a podcast, newsletter, publisher or public commentator | Public source, format and evidence of relevant coverage |

A directory listing is a research route. It does not imply election coverage, endorsement, consent or an instruction to contact anyone. An outlet's suggested deadline is part of its request until Luke chooses a commitment. Keep general factual information distinct from political profiling or demographic persuasion.

## If Luke chooses to develop a draft

Useful context includes the exact text or asset, sources, unverified points, rights, destination and current authorisation status. Dates remain blank unless relevant and deliberately chosen. A draft can remain a draft, be revised or be left alone.

Luke's current explicit instruction governs any external action. Preparing a draft, approving wording or saving a possibility does not by itself authorise a send or post. If an action is authorised and carried out, record the version, destination and actual result. A draft is not a sent message, and a local status change is not delivery evidence.

## Optional factual introduction

This is example wording for review, not a publication instruction:

> Luke Nathan Hayes is exploring a possible mayoral campaign in Redland City. His election explorer brings public media routes, existing work and source links together, with space to save possibilities. Luke chooses the direction and pace, with AI assistance for research and organisation. Nomination certification has not been confirmed.

Before any chosen use, check that the wording still matches the configuration and source record. Add the verified public URL, actual contact route and any required authorisation appropriate to that use. Do not imply an operating contact form when submission remains unavailable.

## Optional handoff notes

A concise handoff can preserve the question or possibility, public sources, exact draft or file path, open points and any decision Luke has actually made. Include recipients only when a direct communication is being considered, and keep private recipient details out of public GitHub. Record a send or publication only after its result is verified.
