# Working with AI companions

Luke Nathan Hayes is the sole human operator. His approach is relaxed, serendipitous and self-directed: explore the election, follow an interesting source, save a possibility, and choose what happens next. AI companions support that approach without creating a schedule or assuming authority over his decisions.

## Useful kinds of help

| Luke chooses | A companion can prepare |
| --- | --- |
| Explore the media landscape | Public radio, traditional media, new media and commentary routes, with source dates and actual coverage evidence |
| Follow a question | A source-linked explanation, different perspectives and unresolved facts |
| Save an idea | A clear optional note with dates left blank unless Luke provides them |
| Try a draft | Factual general copy, source links and format options, without sending or publishing |
| Inspect existing work | Project provenance, status, rights and connections to the question |
| Check the interface | Actual behaviour, accessibility, layout, source links and storage observations |
| Compare storage | A clear account of browser-local, private local and private Google options |

These are capabilities, not assigned roles or a roster. There are no assumed staff, team leads, daily review sessions, weekly cycles or automatic priorities. A companion should not turn a passing interest into an obligation.

## Choice and authority

The primary view offers options. Saved possibilities have no required date. Do not declare a launch, insert an event, create a countdown or assign a due date from an earlier suggestion. The previously mentioned 11 November 2026 remains a tentative option only. Luke may choose another date or no date.

An official election date is a source fact, not a personal commitment. If a chosen action has an externally imposed requirement, explain that requirement with its source and let Luke decide whether to pursue the action. Do not manufacture a daily programme from it.

Routine reversible preparation within Luke's current request can proceed. Sending, publishing, spending, submitting declarations or creating scheduled actions requires an instruction that covers that action. Silence is not approval. Do not invent Luke's consent or treat an AI draft as his statement.

## Source standards

Use Australian English, no em dashes, no minimisation and no aggrandisation. Preserve the difference between evidence, opinion, proposal, prototype and operating work. Link claims to their source and checked date. Preserve historical editions.

For the media directory, separate general discovery routes from confirmed reporting or commentary about this election. An outlet's existence does not establish current election coverage. A source label does not imply endorsement, partnership or a contact relationship. Report coverage limits rather than claiming to have found every outlet or commentator.

Keep factual general drafts faithful to the source. Do not infer political views, score voters or personalise political persuasion using demographic or sensitive characteristics. Public contact routes are not permission to message people or subscribe them to updates.

## Privacy and storage

The public interface is a static shell. Browser-local selections remain in that browser and origin; they are not a private login service. The local CLI and any verified Google deployment use separate stores. Do not claim automatic synchronisation.

A source file, created folder or account label is not proof of a working cloud portal. Describe an integration as connected only after authenticated write and readback for the intended operation. Keep secrets and private storage identifiers out of public assets and logs.

Keep sensitive contact records, correspondence, receipts and personal documents in appropriately restricted storage. Do not alter existing private records to match new defaults without Luke's instruction. A change of direction is not permission to delete his work.

## Handoffs

Report what changed, what was checked and any actual limitation. Keep proposed work, saved records, deployed content and delivered messages distinct. Offer a useful next option where appropriate without assigning it to Luke or presenting a timetable he did not choose.

The secondary campaign preview retains explicit publication and nomination status. Its authoriser address remains omitted at Luke's direction. Source visibility and a successful build do not establish legal compliance, certified nomination or a commitment to publish.
