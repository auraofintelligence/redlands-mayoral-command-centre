# Music airplay and appearances

Updated 10 November 2026, Australia/Brisbane.

Luke wants a record of every known song play, time and appearance during the campaign for ethical overcompliance. The Music airplay log keeps this record privately, alongside evidence and the actual airtime arrangement. No station offers or plays have been inferred from the directory or interview discussion.

## One occurrence per record

Use one row for each song each time it airs. Record interviews and features separately. An album feature does not establish that every track was played; request a track-level playlist or transmission log. A later repeat gets another record. Simultaneous radio and online streaming can be one simulcast record.

Record station, programme, song or item title, version/edit, local broadcast date and time, IANA time zone, time accuracy, duration where known, platform, evidence, arrangement and review status. Australia/Brisbane is the default time zone. Use the station's actual zone, including daylight saving where applicable, rather than converting an unknown time into a guessed one.

Keep an unknown date or time blank. A confirmed broadcast can have an unknown start time if the date and occurrence are evidenced. The interface makes that missing time visible.

## Status and evidence

- Reported: information to verify, including a listener report.
- Scheduled: an appearance or play planned but not confirmed as aired.
- Confirmed: a broadcast date plus station-log, station-confirmation, recording or public-playlist evidence, with a reference or link.
- Cancelled: retained separately from broadcasts that occurred.

Only confirmed song entries count in the confirmed-plays display. Other totals explicitly describe reported, scheduled or confirmed non-song items. These are counts of recorded entries, not a statement of complete coverage or audience reach.

## Financial review remains separate

Record known arrangements as unknown, editorial, paid advertising, paid sponsorship or offered support. These labels describe evidence, not a legal determination. Keep a review note and link any actual finance record after assessment. A logged play does not automatically create a gift, expense, advertising valuation or ECQ return. The public finance export does not include broadcast records or private evidence.

## Coverage and sharing

The local workspace does not continuously monitor stations, search inboxes, send requests or receive public contact submissions automatically. Complete coverage depends on transmission logs, playlists, recordings or confirmed reports. Ask for logs through the ordinary approved contact process, then record the evidence. Do not claim a complete list of plays without reconciling the available sources.

The public media kit has a broadcaster note and blank CSV template. Its contact link opens a media enquiry with a log-request outline. A station's existing log is also suitable. Public messages still require explicit instructions to send; browsing or preparing records grants no sending authority.

Use Export private airplay CSV for review. Store exports and evidence in the ignored private folder. Private interview notes and drafts also remain there. No personal records are bundled into the application build.
